Starshot

IkonSpiritTranscendence
Source War of Immortals pg. 46
Usage a ranged weapon

Immanence

Strikes with the starshot deal an additional 1 spirit splash damage per weapon damage die.

Transcendence — Giant-Felling Comet

You shoot the starshot, causing a detonation in a 5-foot burst within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must succeed at a basic Reflex save against your class DC or take spirit damage equal to your normal Strike damage with the starshot. Creatures larger than you take a –2 circumstance penalty to their saving throws.

Toxicologist

Source Player Core 2 pg. 62
The art of the poisoner: a refined toolkit of injuries and elixirs.

Formulas

Two common 1st-level alchemical poisons.

Field Benefit

You can Activate an injury poison as a single action, provided you apply it to a weapon you're holding or a piece of ammunition on your person or loaded in a weapon you're holding. In addition, you flexibly mix acidic and poisonous alchemical compounds.

BlizzardHazard 5

ColdEnvironmental
Source GM Core pg. 524
A blizzard is a severe winter storm that combines high winds, heavy snow, and bitter cold. Travelers caught in a blizzard face multiple compounding threats simultaneously.

Cold Damage Creatures in the blizzard take 4d6 cold damage every hour unless they have cold resistance or appropriate protection.
Reduced Visibility The driving snow reduces visibility to 10 feet. Creatures beyond this range have the hidden condition.
Difficult Terrain Accumulated snow and ice turn the entire area into difficult terrain. Drifts deeper than 3 feet become greater difficult terrain.
Strong Winds Winds of 35+ mph apply the effects of strong winds: ranged attacks beyond 10 feet take a –4 circumstance penalty.

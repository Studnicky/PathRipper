Mosquito Witch

Rare
Source Divine Mysteries pg. 299
Your patron is a mysterious being known as the Mosquito Witch. You do not know why you were chosen.

Spell List primal
Patron Skill Nature

Lesson of the Unbridled Hive The Mosquito Witch grants you control over insects. You gain the buzzing bites hex cantrip and your familiar learns pest form.
Familiar of Swarm's Heart Your familiar is surrounded by a cloud of harmless insects. When you Cast or Sustain a hex, the insects swarm to protect you and your allies.

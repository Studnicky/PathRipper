Mummy RotCurse 11

CurseDisease
Source Player Core pg. 442
Saving Throw DC 28 Will; Onset 1 day; Maximum Duration 2d6 days
The cursed victim's body slowly rots away. The afflicted creature can't recover from the drained condition while affected by mummy rot.

Stage 1 drained 2 (1 day); Stage 2 drained 3 and the victim's skin begins to crack and dry (1 day); Stage 3 drained 4 and the victim crumbles to dust upon death (1 day).

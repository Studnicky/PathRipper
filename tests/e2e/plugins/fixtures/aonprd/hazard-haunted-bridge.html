Haunted Bridge Haunt

Source Gamemastery Guide pg. 77
Complexity Simple
Stealth DC 20 (trained)
Description A restless spirit haunts an old stone bridge, attacking anyone who dares to cross.
Disable DC 20 Diplomacy (trained) to appease the spirit
AC 16; Fort +8; Ref +5
HP 40
Immunities critical hits, object immunities, precision damage
Screaming Strike Trigger A creature steps onto the bridge; Effect The spirit shrieks and slams into the triggering creature, dealing 2d6+5 bludgeoning damage (DC 20 basic Fortitude save).
Reset The haunt deactivates and then resets after 1 minute.
